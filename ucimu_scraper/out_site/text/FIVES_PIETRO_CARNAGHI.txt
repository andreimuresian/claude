With over 100 years of direct experience in the field, led by Carnaghi and Radice families, in 2025 the company entered into Fives Group, a leading global industrial engineering group with a turnover of € 2.3 billion and a workforce of over 9,200 employees in 2024. Pietro Carnaghi is nowadays a key pillar of High Precision Machines (“HPM”) division of Fives. Two global, customer-centric, market-leading players together to expand markets, customers, and capabilities, to the benefits of their clients. Together, the operations of the HPM division of Fives and Pietro Carnaghi are expected to generate revenues of approximately € 400-450 million/year and rely on a workforce of 1,900 employees across the globe. Pietro Carnaghi, specialized in high-performance, high-quality machine tools, manufactures: - VERTICAL LATHES WITH TURNING AND MILLING CAPACITY UP TO 5 AXES - GANTRY MILLING MACHINES WITH MOVABLE PORTAL - FLEXIBLE MANUFACTURING CELLS – FMS - MULTITASKING MACHINING CENTERS WITH VERTICAL APPROACH – FLEXTURN VERTICAL LATHES With table diameters from 1000 up to 4000 mm, fully customizable. LARGE VERTICAL LATHES “Heavy-duty” configuration machines designed to maximize productivity on large parts up to 17 meters. GANTRY MILLING MACHINES WITH MOVABLE PORTAL Pietro Carnaghi has been producing vertical gantry-type milling machines for several decades — the well-known UNIMILL models. FMS PALLET SYSTEMS Flexible manufacturing system designed to connect multiple machines, through pallets with movable shuttle system. The systems are designed on customers demand and are capable to include also machines from third-party manufacturers, fully integrated with cell X controlling software. FLEXTURN VERTICAL MACHINING CENTER Vertical lathe concept with top milling performance. Pietro Carnaghi’s multitasking solution with vertical RAM and 5 axis interpolations. VIDEO GALLERY Maximum Reliability in All Sectors The environment is always carefully considered — this is clear even just by walking through the company’s production departments. In addition to being a member of UCIMU and embracing a Green philosophy, the company operates in compliance with ISO 9001, ISO 45001 and ISO 14001 standards. To maintain high levels of productivity, diagnostic times and the speed with which technical interventions are organized are key features that distinguish Pietro Carnaghi’s after-sales service. The organization, with its 100 years of experience and installations all over the world, allows us to have a team of specialized technicians always available and who can promptly reach our customers’ premises from the reporting of the fault. Please contact Pietro Carnaghi Service Department here for any information. Assistance Remote Service/Teleservice Pietro Carnaghi offers its customers remote connection systems that allow a real-time machine diagnosis and programming troubleshooting. Supplying a remote service means having the possibility to connect to Pietro Carnaghi machines wherever they are in the world, via secure connections compliant with current regulations. Remote Diagnosis An innovative tool that allows the machine’s operator panel to be viewed directly on a PC in the after-sales office. An important aid for both production and programming. Spare parts The availability of spare parts is essential for minimizing machine downtime. To this aim, Pietro Carnaghi ensures rapid procurement and shipment of spare parts worldwide and, upon request, the establishment of dedicated spare parts warehouses. Preventive Maintenance This is an important concept in which Pietro Carnaghi firmly believes. Scheduled and ongoing maintenance of the machine significantly increases production capacity, drastically reducing downtime by identifying and managing latent issues that may arise over time. The After-Sales Service offers preventative maintenance programs specifically designed for its customers. Technical Training Pietro Carnaghi provides a highly qualified technical staff dedicated to training technicians who will operate Pietro Carnaghi machines. The main courses offered are: Machine Operator Courses Programming Courses Mechanical Maintenance Courses Electrical Maintenance Courses PLC/CNC Programming Courses Pietro Carnaghi Machine Relocation and Upgrades Reorganizing your workshop or simply purchasing used machinery often requires disassembly, relocation, and assembly at different locations. Pietro Carnaghi supports its customers with the relocation of its machines worldwide, providing its expertise and ensuring safety, speed, and the quality that distinguishes it. After several years of intensive production, the machines may require updates with new technologies available on the market and/or retrofitting to restore their quality and safety standards.
