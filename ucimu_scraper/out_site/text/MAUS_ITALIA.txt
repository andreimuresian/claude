Advanced portable product, suitable for complex applications. Stands out for its reliability, precision, ease of use and robustness. In-house design and construction has allowed this product to be optimised, providing a high quality system, composed by: A Giotto FC 250 orbital head for tube to tubesheet TIG welding Giotto PG 300 programmer/generator with microprocessor inverter complete with cooling unit and touchscreen interface Accessories, options and spare parts to meet every need for welding tubes to the tubesheet Download catalog Watch full video Thrust device mounted on the welding head allow welding of flush or protruding tubes and, when necessary, for welding in a protected atmosphere The electrode holder system with preset angles adapts to any welding geometry and makes setup easy and repeatable NEW Argo clamp: TIG orbital welding head centring and locking system Focus features Prime technology for tube to tubesheet junctions In-house design and manufacture has enabled us to optimise the design in order to offer a high-quality system Remote control with display Simple and intuitive interface for control of welding parameters. Comes complete with all manual controls for the welding head. Sectors The division into up to 9 sectors of the welding cycle allows compensation for the negative effects of gravity on the welding puddle. 24/7 Use The enhanced cooling system, inside the head, allows intensive use of the system. Adjustement with a singe key All adjustement available for the different welding geometries only require the single supplied key. Tube to tubesheet TIG orbital welding head Giotto 250B The Giotto 250B head has a device for adjusting the distance of the electrode from the tubesheet. Complete range of devices for centring in the tube consisting of HS cartridge spindles and HC self-centring cartridges. Thanks to components with predetermined angles, the proposed system for each welding geometry is absolute and repeatable. It facilitates geometric setup operations as well as being extremely stable and robust. Digital programmer/generator with inverter with integrated-control microprocessor and digital interface on monitor Giotto PG 300 Powerful and compact, Maus Italia's offers the Giotto PG 300 programmer/generator for controlling the TIG Giotto FC 250 orbital welding head to guarantee 100% repeatability, mounted on "carriage pickUp" with gas bottle holder. Thanks to the integrated 12.1" monitor, backlit in colors (capacitive touch - screen) the parameter programming is extremely fast and simple. TIG orbital welding head centring and locking system Argo Clamp / Head locking Argoclamp centering tool ensures centring and precise support of the head to the tube to be welded. Maus Italia, thanks to experience of its already patented products using radial caliper expanding tools, fits this technology directly on the Giotto FC 250 welding head. Argoclamp instantly expands inside the tube to be welded, locking the orbital head. The Argoclamp is powered by the protective gas dedicated to the torch electrode.
